Lobby

Create or join by room code. Host Start opens a 2-4P lockstep mesh over WebRTC (6 DataChannels at 4P). Delay 2 on the Internet, or 1 with ?ff_lan=1. Each remote client gets a full-frame Hor+ camera for its seat. Host disconnect ends the match. Click the picture to lock the mouse; click again to fire (or Z/Space). Aim is the + at screen center. Hold Shift to run. ?ff_netplay=0 is solo. Campaign is not v1.

Privacy: the ROM never leaves this tab (IndexedDB only; not uploaded through nginx). WebRTC can reveal your IP to peers in the room. Default ICE is all (direct or STUN first). This box has coturn TURN (ephemeral room-scoped creds, no open allocate). ?ff_turnForce=1 forces the TURN relay path; ?ff_wsRelay=1 (or ICE fail after Start) carries inp/ctl on same-origin /ws instead. Keep this tab visible -- hidden tabs stall the match. No analytics SDK. No server browser.
